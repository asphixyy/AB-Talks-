What is ABTalks?

Empowering the Next Generation of Tech Leaders

ABTalks is a non-profit organization based in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, driven by a singular mission: to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world employability.

We believe that consistency is the ultimate currency in the tech industry. Through our flagship 60-Day Coding Challenge, we provide a structured, high-energy environment for college students to build, learn, and showcase their skills publicly.

We are here to turn quiet coders into visible, confident creators. By committing to daily progress and sharing it with the world, our students don't just learn to code—they build a public portfolio that top recruiters simply can't ignore.
